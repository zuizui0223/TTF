# Historical host-memory predictor-only ecological interpretation (2026-10-10)

Status: **exploratory descriptive ecological predictor audit; no genetic response has been opened**.

## What the frozen climate predictor says

Exact source: 71,520 edge rows for 641 eligible Lepidoptera in
`historical-host-memory-full-predictor-v0.1`, Actions artifact 11559015421
from the successful exact frozen eight-raster CHELSA workflow.

`M_host = D_host_LGM - D_host_0BP` is the distance of each present-day
sampled geographic edge to the **present-day host climate cloud**, evaluated
under LGM and current climate. A positive value means that this present
location resembles currently recorded host plants' preferred climate **less
during the LGM** than today. It does not claim that any specific host plant
occupied this location during the LGM.

The response-blind descriptive summary:
- Development: 315/320 species have positive **mean** M_host.
- Confirmatory: 318/321 have positive **mean** M_host.
- Combined: **633/641**, or 98.8%, have positive species-average M_host.
- Median within-species edge correlation of M_host and M_self:
  development **0.839**, confirmatory **0.845**.
- Frozen earlier nonredundant host history after controlling M_self and
  current host mismatch: median unique fractions **0.235** in development
  and **0.241** in confirmatory.

These facts are **not** evidence that gene flow was disrupted by host
fragmentation during the LGM.

## Ecological implications and limits

The near-universal positive sign of mean M_host is compatible with broad
ice-age climate displacement relative to modern host climate. Its sign is
not the empirical estimand. The predeclared genetic question is whether,
**within a species**, variation among its geographic edges in M_host
predicts mitochondrial post-IBD genetic turnover, after the frozen
insect-own-history and present-host-distance controls.

The strong predictor correlation (roughly 0.84 as the median of species
within-edge correlations) means historical **biotic resource opportunity**
must be carefully distinguished from the insect's own abiotic climatic
exposure. Surviving predictor uniqueness is an evaluability criterion,
not proof of a biological interaction.

The modern WCVP/HOSTS dataset is not an LGM insect–plant association
archive. Existing response-blind review flags identify 12 of 642
candidate insects with specific cultivated-host temporal concerns,
but these are not a post-hoc exclusion rule and do not validate the
historical interactions for the other taxa. The host model is a climate
**opportunity proxy**, not a reconstructed time-indexed interaction network.

The model is mitochondrial COI-based; any future positive result would
require caution in generalizing to genome-wide migration or fitness.
No post-result predictor swapping, species removal, subgroup rescue, or
ecological effect claim is permitted.
