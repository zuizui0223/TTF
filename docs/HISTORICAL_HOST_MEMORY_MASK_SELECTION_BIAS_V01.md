# Historical host-memory mask attrition: scope and ecological generalizability

**Exploratory response-blind sampling audit — 2026-10-11.**
No confirmatory nucleotide identities, genetic distances, turnover measures
or host-history genetic coefficients were opened to produce this note.
The panel and all scientific thresholds are unchanged.

## Original 321-species confirmatory source versus fixed 259-species survivor set

The pre-mask confirmatory sample contains 321 species and 41,592 frozen
genetic-geography edges. The previously frozen `>=50%` jointly canonical
site rule requires **every original graph edge** of a species to have a
valid cross-locality sequence pair. The exact private mask replay retained
259 species (80.69% of the original species) and **20,362 original edges
(48.96%)**. Another 62 species with 21,230 original edges were dropped
in their entirety, not edge-by-edge.

The total of 521 edges failing canonical overlap is the count for
mask-checkable species; one malformed alignment cannot supply a
per-edge validity tally. **21,230 excluded original edges** must not
be misdescribed as 21,230 character-mask failures. They were removed
as part of whole-species ineligibility.

## Mask inclusion is not uniform

This is a descriptive post-mask comparison, not an additional eligibility
criterion or confirmatory hypothesis test.

| Original frozen edges per species | Species | Valid-mask survivors | Inclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–49 | 176 | 154 | 87.5% |
| 50–99 | 59 | 47 | 79.7% |
| 100–199 | 52 | 38 | 73.1% |
| 200–499 | 22 | 16 | 72.7% |
| 500 or more | 12 | 4 | 33.3% |

The survivor median original graph has **43** edges, whereas the failed-species
median has **78.5** edges; the corresponding frozen locality counts are
21 vs 28.5. A greater number of frozen edges makes the all-edges-pass mask
criterion more demanding, so unequal attrition is a methodological feature
of the frozen rule, not direct evidence of any biological characteristic.

Mask survival is also heterogeneous across large families: `Gracillariidae`
19/39 (48.7%), `Noctuidae` 51/53 (96.2%), `Tortricidae` 33/36
(91.7%), and `Geometridae` 28/36 (77.8%). These are descriptive
differences and **not** prespecified evolutionary subgroup results.
The underlying mask failures may reflect genetic marker coverage and
sampling geometry; we must not claim family-specific historical host
effects from these counts.

## What does and does not change

The median **host-memory unique predictor fraction** is close in surviving
and failed species: 0.24059 vs 0.23971. Thus, the visibly heterogeneous mask
eligibility is not accompanied by a large median difference in that
specific external climatic predictor diagnostic. This does **not** prove
absence of selection bias in other traits, taxa, geography, marker coverage
or eventual genetic responses.

The original response-blind plan remains locked:
- The **259 original surviving species** and their **20,362 unchanged edges**
  define the one-shot confirmatory estimand.
- Every original survivor was retained; no alternate locus, species
  backfill, repaired edge, or post-result family exclusion is allowed.
- The eligible subset already passed the unchanged predictor information
  and independent synthetic Type-I/power gates.
- Actual genetic divergence, current gene flow, historical dispersal or
  ecological causation have **not** been assessed.

The eventual paper must distinguish statistical detectability **within the
259 eligible COI-panel species** from ecological generalizability to the
original 321 Lepidoptera or all insects. Raw COI variation alone cannot
separate historical migration from drift, selection and sampling.
