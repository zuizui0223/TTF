# Codistributed recurrence — result synthesis v0.1

Status: **one-shot confirmatory result complete; no post-result retuning**

## One question

> Does sharing more of the same landscape make post-IBD phylogeographic structure more recurrent across lineages?

## One answer

> **No. Greater shared geographic opportunity did not make spatial genetic structure more reusable across the qualified insect lineages.**

The confirmatory empirical coefficient was `beta_G = -0.004447`, with a least-favourable calibrated envelope `p = 0.969`.

This is the prospectively frozen **qualified-null** branch, not an exploratory reinterpretation.

## Why this is biologically informative rather than an uninformative null

Three independent conditions were satisfied before the empirical response was opened.

1. **The relation existed in the geometry.**  
   After exact source and target identity plus frozen sampling, taxonomy and centroid controls, the survivor relation retained a unique variance fraction of **0.1982**. Residual signal was distributed broadly across endpoints rather than concentrated in a few species.

2. **The estimator was calibrated on the realized geometry.**  
   Private-world rejection rates were **0.048, 0.000, 0.004 and 0.046** across the four frozen private amplitudes, with all Wilson 95% upper bounds below 0.10.

3. **A shared-place signal of the frozen positive-control magnitude was detectable.**  
   The positive control was rejected in **473/500 worlds (power 0.946; Wilson lower 0.9226)**.

The null therefore cannot be dismissed as a missing predictor, collapsed design, or obviously powerless test.

## What changed relative to the completed 211-species result

The completed broad result showed that post-IBD spatial genetic structure was not detectably portable to unseen species.

The simplest ecological objection was:

> perhaps species-specific fields failed to transfer because the source and target lineages did not occupy enough of the same geography.

The fresh codistributed experiment tested that objection directly and prospectively. It failed.

Therefore the broad non-transfer result is **not explained simply by lack of shared geographic opportunity**.

## The ecological interpretation

Raw transfer skill varied strongly among source-target pairs (`T_st` SD **0.313**, range **-0.924 to 0.941**). There is therefore real heterogeneity to explain.

But the amount of mutually sampled landscape did not explain that heterogeneity in the predicted positive direction.

The most defensible biological statement is:

> **The same sampled landscape need not have the same effective phylogeographic meaning for different lineages.**

Physical geography can still be important within lineages. What fails is the stronger assumption that the effect of place is sufficiently lineage-invariant to be carried from one species to another simply because both species share more of that place.

This shifts the mechanism problem from **place alone** to **lineage × place**.

Candidate mechanisms include dispersal, habitat dependence, historical occupancy, demographic response and other lineage-specific processes that determine how a geographic feature is translated into genetic structure. These are future hypotheses, not explanations established by the current result.

## What this result does not show

Do not claim that:

- geography is unimportant;
- mountains, rivers or other physical barriers do not matter;
- all phylogeographic structure is lineage-specific;
- geographic co-opportunity has a negative causal effect;
- no biologically coherent subset can share barriers;
- mitochondrial structure necessarily generalizes to nuclear genomic structure;
- the result is universal across Animalia.

## Paper spine

### Problem

Comparative phylogeography often treats geographic concordance as evidence that places or barriers repeatedly structure lineages.

### Missing test

Retrospective concordance does not establish that spatial information learned from one lineage predicts an unseen lineage. A major alternative explanation for weak transfer is simply that lineages did not share enough geography.

### Prospective test

Measure mutual geographic co-opportunity before response opening, hold source and target lineages disjoint, calibrate the mechanical benefits of shared geometry with lineage-private worlds, and ask whether excess out-of-lineage transfer increases with co-opportunity.

### Result

It does not.

### Biological conclusion

> **Phylogeographic structure is not made portable by shared landscape opportunity alone.**

The next explanatory level is lineage-dependent translation of geography rather than a universal map of effective barriers.

## Integrity boundary for successor work

The confirmatory genetic response for these 326 species is now open. It must not be mined to choose a favourable dispersal, habitat, climatic, historical or taxonomic mechanism and then reported as confirmatory.

Any successor test of a lineage-by-place mechanism must be one of:

- a prospectively frozen predictor evaluated in a genuinely fresh response panel;
- an external independent dataset;
- an explicitly exploratory analysis whose result is not promoted to confirmatory evidence.

No subgroup rescue or alternate relation is authorized for the current one-shot result.
